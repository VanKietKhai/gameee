This allows admins to create as many Realms as needed without issue.\r\n\r\nSo why is the player limit capped at two Realms per slot - and not three or four?\r\n\r\nFirst, this is due to how Conan’s land-claim system works. When a player claims land by building, the claim applies to a specific X/Y area, but it extends infinitely in height. Even if you travel tens of thousands of meters above your base, the land claim still applies. Because of this infinite vertical ownership, only two stacked Realms can safely exist per slot without causing conflicts with the map’s height limits. Second, it’s also a matter of efficient slot usage. Allowing players to build four Realms would consume two full slots, potentially taking space that could otherwise support two Realms for another player. On busy servers, this would significantly reduce the number of players who can have Realms.\r\n\r\nAllowing three Realms creates a similar inefficiency: it still occupies two slots, but only three Realms are placed, leaving one position unused. That empty space could instead be used to host two Realms for another player, rather than being wasted.\r\n\r\nFor these reasons - technical limitations and fair, efficient space distribution - the system limits players to two Realms per slot.'
