F. DEATHTRAUMA (them 09/10/2026)  - Settings > Server > Administration, hoac console (admin)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Muc tieu: giu do kho 8/10. Server da kho san (PlayerDamageTaken 1.15, NPCDamage 1.35,
roi tui do khi chet, phuc kich Night Terrors + PvE Ambush 30%) nen Trauma o muc NHE HON mac dinh.
Go "dc trauma status" truoc va sau de xem don vi thuc te (phut / %).
  Thoi gian Trauma ................. dc trauma duration 20      (mac dinh 30 phut)
  Giam chi so moi cap .............. dc trauma loss 10          (mac dinh 15%)
  Mau/the luc toi thieu ............ dc trauma minstats 70      (toi da ~3 cap anh huong)
  Doi/khat toi thieu ............... dc trauma minneeds 70
  Ve xac de go Trauma .............. dc trauma recovery on      (khuyen khich chay ve xac lay tui do)
  Cach tinh gio .................... dc trauma timer corpse     (chet lien tiep khong reset gio)
  Tu sat / thao vong tay ........... giu mac dinh: 2 cap, x2 thoi gian (chong lach luat)
  Grace sau khi hoi sinh ........... dc trauma grace on  +  dc trauma grace duration 30
  Thuoc chua Trauma ................ dc trauma potion tier1     (can KHOI DONG LAI server)
  Danh dau xac ..................... giu mac dinh ON / 100 m
DBNO (cuu dong doi): dung DBNO cua DeathTrauma, TAT Player DBNO cu
  Death DBNO ....................... BAT (trong bang Administration), thoi gian 90 s
  Giu phim cuu ..................... 5 s ; mau sau khi cuu 25%
  KO DBNO .......................... TAT
  Sau khi bat Death DBNO: go "dc toggleplayerdbno" de TAT Player DBNO (tranh 2 he thong cung chay).
  Ly do chon DeathTrauma: cung dieu kien (dong doi trong ban kinh), them nut Bo cuoc (Give Up),
  cuu bang giu phim 5 s, chinh duoc mau sau khi cuu, va duoc cuu thi KHONG bi Trauma.
